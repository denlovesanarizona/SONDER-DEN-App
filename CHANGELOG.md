# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-06)
- Summary events (imported history) show "Summary" or a month range instead of a clock time.
- Summaries no longer count toward streaks, the heatmap, or entry/log totals.
- Exports show summaries without a made-up time.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-04)
- Entries and quick logs can be back-dated: tap the Date & time field (or the calendar pill in Quick Log) to pick any past day and time.
- Mood emojis replaced with custom icons from /icons (lotus-flower, positivity, mirror, sun, sleep). Icons follow the light/dark theme.
- "Journaling since" now reflects your earliest entry, including back-dated ones.
- About screen shows what's new in each version.
- Service worker also caches the mood icons for offline use.

## 1.0.0
- First release: Home, Timeline, Editor, Quick Log, Insights, Account, backup/restore, PIN lock, installable PWA.
